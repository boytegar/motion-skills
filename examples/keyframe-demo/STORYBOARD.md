# STORYBOARD — "Retiming is a number"

Engine: kyfram. Style: papercut. 5 scenes, ~30 s, 1080x1920 @ 30 fps.

## HOOK

```
SAY:   You change one number and the whole cut moves.
WRITE: ONE NUMBER
SHOW:  frame 0: the paper hero already mid-slide, a torn tag stamped "1" over it,
       the camera 4% in and pushing. No fade-in, no empty room.
ALIGN: retiming is a number, not a rewrite
```

The three layers differ in words, never in subject: the tag says ONE NUMBER, the
voice says you change one number, the picture shows a single tag doing all the work.

- S03 → S04: the plan card flips over as the counter.
