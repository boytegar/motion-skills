# Agent Brief: Bang Motion

Guidelines for creating scenes in `bang-motion` style:
- Never static slides. Keep the camera breathing (1.0 -> 1.05 scale).
- One primary statement per scene, followed by a focal UI card or object.
- Use `GlowHorizon` as the background, not flat black or white.
- Wrap content in `GlassCard` or let `KineticTitle` take center stage.
