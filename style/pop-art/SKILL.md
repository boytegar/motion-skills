# Pop Art
Almost every banana in the shops is one plant, copied: a wall of identical prints, an old banana eaten black by dots, and a wave of colour that turns into many bananas.

Imported from [Lemo-Opuscar by Lemomo (LemoLab)](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar) (`styles/pop-art`), CC BY-NC 4.0. Style content is unchanged; only the file layout was adapted.

**Planning only.** This pack ships the docs and tokens, no render kit of its own. `motion-strategy`, `motion-hook`, `motion-script` and `motion-storyboard` work normally: read `docs/style-guide.md` for the DNA and `docs/style-prompt.md` for portable prompts.

## Files
